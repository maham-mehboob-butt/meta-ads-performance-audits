## 📊 Campaign Performance Comparison

| Performance Metric | Campaign 1 (Single Product) | Campaign 2 (Multi-Product Lineup) | Strategic Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Status** | Active | Active | Continuous Scaling |
| **Total Spend** | PKR 44,156.43 (538 Days) | PKR 68,296.66 (522 Days) | +54.6% Budget Allocation |
| **Messaging Conversations** | 3,017 | **8,298** | **+175.0% Inquiry Volume** |
| **Cost Per Lead (CPL)** | **Rs 14.64** | **Rs 8.23** | **43.8% Cost Reduction** |
| **Total Views / Impressions**| 505K | **1.0M (1,000,000)** | +98.0% Ad Impression Growth |
| **Reach** | 365.4K Accounts | **700.7K Accounts** | +91.7% Organic Amplification |
| **Link Clicks** | 5,098 Clicks | **12,257 Clicks** | +140.4% High-Intent Traffic |

---

## 🎯 Key Strategic Takeaways

1. **Creative Diversity Drive Conversions:** Transitioning to a multi-item product lineup reduced messaging lead costs from **Rs 14.64** down to **Rs 8.23** per chat.
2. **Intent-Driven Copywriting:** Actionable value propositions (*"Step with Confidence & Style!"*) yielded **12,257 link clicks** and higher WhatsApp order intent.
3. **Budget Reallocation:** Shifting daily ad spend toward Campaign 2 expanded reach to **700.7K accounts** while lowering customer acquisition costs.
